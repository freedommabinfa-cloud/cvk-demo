Trust, made verifiable.

Off-grid residents can't produce a utility bill or tenancy agreement, so they stay stuck at Tier 2. CVK lets gazetted traditional and religious authorities attest to a resident's address, and the bank accepts that attestation as alternative proof of address. It works on top of BVN/NIN and does not replace them.

Tier 2 to Tier 3

The problem

CBN rules require proof of address (a utility bill or tenancy agreement) to upgrade to Tier 3. Residents without grid access can't produce one, which locks them out of loans, higher transaction limits and uncapped balances.

Built on BVN/NIN

The solution

Gazetted traditional and religious authorities attest to a resident's address. A compliance layer cross-checks each attestor against a gazette registry, flags jurisdiction mismatches and freezes suspected fraud. A 6-month pilot with a microfinance bank is in preparation.
